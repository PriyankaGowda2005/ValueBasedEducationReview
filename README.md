# Value-Based Education and Student Mental Well-Being: A Narrative Literature Review

A comprehensive narrative literature review exploring how traditional Indian value systems (Basavanna's Vachanas, Hitopadesha, Panchatantra) might conceptually support mental health and emotional well-being in school students through a scenario-based analytical approach.

## 📋 Overview

This repository contains a narrative literature review that employs a scenario-based analytical synthesis to examine the potential connections between value-based education grounded in traditional Indian knowledge systems and student mental health outcomes. The review explicitly positions itself as a **conceptual contribution**, not an empirical study, and provides theoretical frameworks for future research.
